# Kirana Ledger Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (fictional)

Kirana Ledger is a Bengaluru-based B2B SaaS and payments company. Its app gives neighbourhood kirana stores and FMCG distributors digital bookkeeping, credit ledgers, UPI collections and inventory reordering.

## Fundraise
The company is raising a Series B of INR 120 crore at an INR 600 crore pre-money valuation. Proceeds will fund expansion into 8 new states and a merchant lending product.

## Management Highlights
- FY2026 revenue of INR 48.0 crore, up 63% year on year.
- EBITDA breakeven achieved in Q4 FY2026.
- Net revenue retention of 128%.
- Over 2,900 paying merchants and 5 enterprise distributor accounts.

## Leadership
Founder and CEO: Meera Krishnan. Co-founder and CTO: Arvind Rao. CFO: Sanjay Kulkarni (joined June 2025).
