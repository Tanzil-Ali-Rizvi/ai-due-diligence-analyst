# Data Protection and Security

Kirana Ledger processes merchant KYC documents (PAN, Aadhaar-masked, GST certificates) and payment transaction data.

## Certifications
ISO 27001 certified since 2024. SOC 2 Type I audit in progress, expected Q1 2027.

## Incidents
In July 2025 a misconfigured cloud storage bucket exposed KYC documents of approximately 12,400 merchants for 9 days. The bucket was secured and access logs were reviewed. Affected merchants were not notified, and the incident was not reported to the Data Protection Board of India or CERT-In.

## Policies
A Data Protection Officer was appointed in March 2026. Data retention follows RBI guidelines for payment data.
