# Management Accounts Summary FY2026

Prepared by management for the Series B data room. Unaudited.

- Total revenue for FY2026: INR 48.0 crore.
- Gross margin: approximately 70%.

Revenue includes subscription fees, payment processing fees and distributor platform fees. Management has included platform GMV fees on a gross basis for FY2026.
