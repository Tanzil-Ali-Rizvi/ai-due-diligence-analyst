# Master Services Agreement: Metro Mart Retail Pvt Ltd

Effective 1 April 2024. Term ends 31 March 2027, renewable by mutual written agreement.

## 4. Services
Kirana Ledger provides its distributor platform, ledger reconciliation and UPI collections to Metro Mart's network of approximately 1,800 franchise stores.

## 9. Fees
Platform fees are charged monthly at 1.1% of gross merchandise value processed, subject to a minimum of INR 1.2 crore per month.

## 12. Data
All transaction data remains the property of Metro Mart. Kirana Ledger must delete Metro Mart data within 30 days of termination.

## 14. Termination
14.1 Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within 45 days.
14.2 Metro Mart may terminate for convenience with 90 days' written notice after 31 March 2026.
14.3 Change of Control. If Kirana Ledger undergoes a change of control, including the acquisition of more than 50% of its voting shares by any third party, Metro Mart may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice, without penalty.

## 18. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India, with courts in Mumbai having exclusive jurisdiction.
